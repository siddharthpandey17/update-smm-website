Our Story

Ek chhoti coaching se, ek bharosemand naam tak

Gaurav Coaching Institute Kanpur mein shuru hui thi ek simple maksad ke saath — computer education aur admission guidance ko har student tak asaan aur affordable banana.

Aaj hum computer basics, NIOS/open board admission counselling aur digital marketing training deते hain — hamesha practical, hands-on approach ke saath, jisse students sirf sikhein nahi balki apply bhi kar sakein.
